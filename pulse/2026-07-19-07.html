Kraken's 'Skinny' Fed Master Account Still Is Not Operational

Fintech Business Weekly

Kraken Financial was approved for a limited-purpose Federal Reserve master account earlier this year, but its bank CEO told a Wyoming legislative committee that the company is still trying to get the account operationalized. That matters because the press-cycle version of the story was 'crypto bank gets direct access to Fed rails'; the operating reality is messier, narrower, and still dependent on policy development.

The deeper point is that payment infrastructure access remains both strategic and political. Tier 3 applicants are nominally eligible, but Fed Vice Chair Michelle Bowman described that path as effectively 'unobtainium,' and the Board is encouraging Reserve Banks to pause decisions while it revises the payment account framework.

My take: this is the exact gap fintech companies keep running into. Distribution and product can move quickly, but access to final settlement, insured deposits, and regulated payment rails is still mediated by institutions and processes designed to move slowly.

"trying to get the account operationalized"

Why it matters: For Hiro, this is a reminder that financial infrastructure strategy is not just API selection. The durable moat often sits at the regulatory and settlement layer.
