Argentina Draws the World Cup's Clearest Fast Lane

Silver Bulletin

Nate Silver's model says Argentina has landed by far the easiest knockout path: Cape Verde in the round of 32, then Australia or Egypt in the round of 16, and no other current top-five side likely before the semifinal. That matters because tournament odds are not just team strength; they are path dependency wearing a sports jersey.

The piece is useful cultural context more than Hiro-core material. The good general lesson is incentives and bracket structure can overwhelm headline rankings. In a probabilistic system, the draw is part of the product.

"Argentina's average opponent, however, has a PELE rating of 1898."

Why it matters: A clean reminder that expected outcomes often move more from path structure than from intrinsic quality. Also useful World Cup small talk.
