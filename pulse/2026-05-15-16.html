Affirm Makes Transformer Models a Credit-Underwriting Baseline

Fintech Takes

Affirm told investors that transformer-based AI models are outperforming its current gradient-boosted underwriting models and are expected to become the new baseline across its credit stack. The important detail is not that Affirm is using fashionable AI language; it is that sequence models may extract repayment signals from raw financial histories without as much hand-engineered feature design.

For consumer finance, this is exactly the technical frontier that regulators will struggle with: better approval/loss curves on one side, explainability and fair-lending replicability on the other. The model advantage is real enough that lenders will feel pressure to adopt it, but the governance layer has to catch up fast.

"the transformer-based model architecture will become the form we're going to be transitioning all of our models to"

Why it matters: Directly relevant to AI financial advice and underwriting: the frontier is moving from hand-built features to learned representations of financial behavior, which changes both product capability and regulatory risk.
